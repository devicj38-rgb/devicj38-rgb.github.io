Selected Works · 02 / 作品

Selected Works.

十二件移动影像 —— 商业广告、悬疑短片、MV 与 MG 动画，导演、拍摄与剪辑均由本人或本人参与的团队制作独立完成。
Twelve moving-image pieces: brand films, shorts, music videos & motion design — directed, shot and cut by me, or by teams I worked with.

  • 01 · Brand Film

    RIO 微醺 — Quiet Hours

    Brand film · Bubbles, breath & a tipsy hour · 2026

    A rhythmic slice for the low-ABV sparkling brand RIO. Built around the mood of a "tipsy moment" — rising bubbles, light on glass, and beat-matched cuts that breathe. Less about drinking, more about holding on to a night that feels just right.

  • 02 · Brand Film

    苏可安 — 一刻柔光

    Commercial · A moment of herbal soft light · 2025

    The full commercial for Suken. Titled "A Moment of Soft Light", it opens with the first natural light of morning, moves into macro textures of the product, and lands on a gentle dialogue between herbal ingredients and skin. Grading deliberately lowers saturation and lifts softness, making "gentle repair" something you can actually see.

  • 03 · Brand Film

    毓婷 — Yumeilii 毓美丽

    Commercial · Softness & resolve · 1′36″ full film

    The full 1'36" brand film for Yuting "Yumeilii". It plays on a dual temperament of softness and resolve — restrained, delicate lensing under a soft-light grade, wrapped around a steady narrative spine. An attempt to give a women's wellness film real strength, outside the usual pastel grammar of the category.

  • 04 · Campus Film

    Campus Promotion Film — 融媒体中心

    Campus film · Seasons, corridors & people · 8′30″ full film

    An 8'30" campus promotion film made for the university media center. Four seasons set the timeline; patient long takes link classrooms, corridors and the real people inside them. The edit keeps deliberate room to breathe — no stacked drone shots, no slogans — letting "studying here" speak for itself.

  • 05 · Short Film

    23:59:59 — Suspense Short

    Suspense short · One minute on the clock · 2025

    A suspense short built on the last minute before midnight. A clock, a door left ajar, light that keeps failing — three elements compressed into a sixty-second countdown. The cutting tightens with every tick of the second hand, probing how the certainty of running out of time turns into dread of the unknown.

  • 06 · AIGC Film

    Graduation Promo — AIGC

    Graduation promo · Full AIGC pipeline · 2026

    A graduation-themed promo produced through a full AIGC pipeline — storyboards, image generation and final assembly all run through an AI toolchain, with two generated segments joined on rhythm. A complete acceptance test of my own AI film workflow: not just using the tools, but making the tools understand the breathing of an edit.

  • 07 · Music Video

    Mystery MV — 推理短剧

    Mystery-drama MV · Rhythm in the shadows · 2K · 2026

    A mystery-drama music video in 2K. Shadow, partial close-ups and alternating fast-slow lensing translate suspense into music visual language: the chorus lands on quick cuts of evidence flashbacks, while the quiet passages are left to a slow push-in. Here deduction is not just plot — it is a grammar of editing.

  • 08 · Music Video

    Anime MV — Motion Visual

    Anime MV · Motion-visual experiment · 2K · 2026

    A 2K anime motion-visual experiment, spliced from two sources: motion illustration and img2video footage. It probes how 2D characters live under real camera logic — push-pulls, depth of field and bokeh invited into the illustrated world. A technical trial at making paper come alive.

  • 09 · Motion Design

    Beijing Landmarks MG — 北京地标

    Motion design · Landmarks drawn in lines · 2025

    An MG animation of Beijing landmarks. Drawn lines and graphic transitions stitch the city together — buildings morph into one another through geometric deformation, and old and new architecture grow from the same sheet of paper. A systematic exercise in motion-design grammar: making information move with order.

  • 10 · Ad Slice

    Pocari Sweat — 宝矿力

    Creative ad slice · Summer, sweat & blue · 2026

    A Pocari Sweat creative slice borrowing the imagery of a Japanese summer: youth, sweat and great fields of blue. The camera rides the rise and fall of a running breath, electrolyte bubbles become the only "effect" on screen — the refill moment shot as a short chronicle of youth.

  • 11 · Behind the Scenes

    Event Flash — 活动快闪

    Highlight edit · Blue-toned flash of energy · 2025

    A blue-toned event highlight edit. One filter unifies the room's color temperature while beat-matched cuts compress time — from doors opening to the last echo of the crowd. The full emotional arc of a live event packed into one short reel; highlights are not leftovers, they are the second main film of the night.

  • 12 · Music Video

    Fruit Band MV — 水果乐队

    Song MV slice · A band & their fruit reverie · 2026

    A slice of a Fruit Band music video. Saturated color and jumpy cuts echo the song's playful reverie — fruit plays instrument, prop and stage lead, performing alongside the band. A seriously unserious film, and my most relaxed edit to date.
